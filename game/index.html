Sizzle Rush

A lunch-rush kitchen game — chop, grill, plate, serve.

Move: WASD / Arrow keys or click a station to walk there  •  Interact: E  •  Chop: hold Space at the board  •  P pause  •  M mute  •  R restart
Click an order ticket to choose which dish you are plating for. Patties burn if left on the grill — grab them when they say READY!
